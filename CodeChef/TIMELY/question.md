## Problem Statement

### Reach on Time

Chef has recently moved into an apartment. It takes 303030 minutes for Chef to reach office from the apartment.

Chef left for the office XXX minutes before Chef was supposed to reach. Determine whether or not Chef will be able to reach on time.

### Input Format

- The first line of input will contain a single integer TTT, denoting the number of test cases.

- Each test case consists of a single integer XXX.

### Output Format

For each test case, output `YES` if Chef will reach on time, `NO` otherwise.

The output is case-insensitive. Thus, the strings `YES`, `yes`, `yeS`, and `Yes` are all considered the same.

### Constraints

- 1≤T≤601 \leq T \leq 601≤T≤60

- 1≤X≤601 \leq X \leq 601≤X≤60

**Test case 1:** Chef leaves 303030 minutes before he is supposed to reach, so he will reach the office exactly on time since it takes 303030 minutes to commute.

**Test case 2:** Chef will reach 303030 minutes early.

**Test case 3:** Chef will reach 16 minutes late.
